What Is Manufacturing ERP?

Manufacturing ERP Software is a centralized system that helps factories plan, execute, monitor, and optimize production processes. Dotone ERP goes beyond traditional ERP by integrating Vision AI, automation, and real-time shop-floor data.

  • ✔ Production Planning & Control
  • ✔ Inventory & Material Management
  • ✔ Quality & Inspection Tracking
  • ✔ Purchase, Sales & Finance
  • ✔ Real-time Factory Dashboards

Core ERP Modules

Production Management

Work orders, BOM, routing, scheduling, WIP tracking, and production output monitoring.

Inventory & Stores

Raw material, semi-finished, finished goods, batch tracking, and stock valuation.

Quality Management

Quality checks, defect tracking, rejection analysis, and AI-based inspection integration.

Purchase & Vendor Management

RFQ, purchase orders, GRN, supplier performance, and cost control.

Sales & Dispatch

Orders, invoicing, dispatch planning, and customer tracking.

Reports & Analytics

Real-time dashboards, KPIs, OEE, productivity, and management insights.
